# Module 6 — Deliver and defend your project

[Project overview](../README.md) · [Workflow](../STUDENT_WORKFLOW.md) · [Rubric PDF](../rubrics/PromptLab_Module_6_Project_Rubric.pdf)

Use your own completed PromptLab repository. There is no new implementation starter or invented Module 6 feature. A supplied rehearsal app cannot replace your assessed project.

1. Check the final project from a clean clone using your documented setup. Commit the complete evidence log and supporting records, and provide the repository link, reviewer access and exact final commit hash through the course's submission channel.
2. Obtain the instructor's written selection of one backend function and one React component at that commit. Record the prompt-set ID and issue time. The recording window is 48 hours from issue.
3. Record 12–20 continuous minutes of screen and audio with code visible, without cuts or another person assisting. You may read the code. Explain what each selected item does, why it exists and the concrete consequence of removing it.
4. Trace a real UI action through client, handler, storage, response and rendered result, using actual names from your source. Defend a design decision.
5. Show a traceable real AI override/rejection and a specific error or unhelpful incident, including its cost. Link the relevant evidence and recording timestamps.

Keep the selected code version stable during review. Deliver the recording link and requested evidence through the course's designated channel. The instructor may ask up to three written clarification questions, due within two business days. Re-attempts use new files; accommodations may change format/timing rather than competence requirements.

**Criteria:** C3.6–C3.8. C3.6 is MUST PASS. Final review also considers the accumulated evidence for Modules 2–5. See ASSESSMENT.md for the retained attempt, return and revision rules.
