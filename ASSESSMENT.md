# Assessment and final demonstration


The final instructor-issued selection names one backend function and one React component at the final
commit. 48 hours from issue;12–20 continuous screen/audio minutes, visible code, no cuts or other person
assisting. Explain what/why/removal-consequence for both items; trace UI→client→handler→storage→response→render;
defend a decision and show a traceable AI override and error/unhelpful incident with its cost. You may read
code. Up to three written questions clarify ambiguous answers, due in two business days. Re-attempts use
new files; accommodations may change format/time, not competence requirements.

The 22 criteria are summarized below and mapped in EVIDENCE.md. No averaging:
all criteria per competency must be Met. C1.4, C2.4, C2.7, C3.3 and C3.6 are MUST PASS; failure returns
the affected evidence group. The five original module groups retain separate three-attempt accounts
within final review; returns do not consume attempts. Revision plans and >=48 hours after feedback are
required; consultation precedes a third attempt. Preserve authentic history rather than reconstructing it.

**Timing caveat:** the original four-hour scaffold-to-first-passing-backend-endpoint budget is unvalidated.
Collect pilot timestamps/blockers; require calibration and criterion versioning before timing-based Not Yet.
Do not automatically award Met for missing calibration. This is not a four-hour budget for the whole app.
